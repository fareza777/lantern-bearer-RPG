Privacy Policy · Effective October 5, 2026

Gloamreach: Chronicle of the Last Lantern is a single-player dark-fantasy RPG. This policy explains what information is handled when you use the Android app.

Information stored on your device

Game saves, settings, and progress are stored locally on your device. Hollow Lantern Games does not require an account and does not operate a game account database. Removing the app or clearing app data can remove local saves; use the in-game export feature before changing devices.

Advertising

The free version may show banner advertising supplied by Google AdMob. Google and its advertising partners may process device identifiers, approximate location, IP address, diagnostics, and ad interaction information to provide, measure, and protect advertising, subject to your choices and applicable law. The app requests the consent choices provided by Google's User Messaging Platform where required.

Remove Ads purchase

Gloamreach offers an optional one-time in-app purchase called Remove Ads for US$4.99 (or the local equivalent shown by Google Play). Google Play processes payment, purchase, and licensing information. Hollow Lantern Games does not receive your full payment-card details. The entitlement is restored through your Google Play account on supported devices.

Diagnostics and support

The app does not intentionally collect names, contacts, precise location, microphone recordings, or photos. If you email the developer for support, the information you choose to include is used only to respond to that request.

Children

Gloamreach is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Third-party services

The app uses Google Play services, Google Mobile Ads, and Google Play Billing. Their handling of information is governed by their own policies and the choices presented in their consent and purchase flows.

Changes

We may update this policy when the app or its services change. The effective date above indicates the latest revision.
