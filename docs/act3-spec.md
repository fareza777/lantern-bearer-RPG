## STORY BIBLE (Act III). Player-facing summary; keep all names/ids exact
Setting recap: Act I Lanternbearer beats Hollow Cantor. Act II: Ivel devoured (heart of the Gloam). Endings before (`ring`,`silence`,`ember`,`hunger`) — whichever you picked, the next dusk you wake in Candlemere. The world does not let its Keys rest.
**Act III opens: The Black Noon.** At midday the sky over Candlemere opens like an eye: a black sun. Everyone can look straight at it. It looks back.
Truth (revealed in stages, twists marked T):
- Long ago the world had a true sun: **the Radiant**. It was not a god, it was a predator: it fed on everything it warmed. The **Nine Lamplighters** (a scholar-smith order) put it to sleep by drowning it in a lid of darkness: the **Gloam**. **T1: the Gloam is not the enemy, it is a lid over a sleeping, hungry sun.** The Ninth Flame was its eye.
- Every lantern (including yours) is a shard of the Radiant's eye, forged by **Kaldrec the Sunforger**, the first Lanternsmith. Every time Lanternbearers kill Gloam things and light lanterns, the Radiant stirs. The Black Noon is its eye opening.
- **T2: You are the Key.** The Lamplighters could not keep the lid on forever: the lid must be re-sealed by a Key: a vessel forged from the Radiant's own ash, with its face taken as the price (this is why you cannot remember your face, and why every Lanternbearer in the game wears hood/mask/veil). Kaldrec made the first Key. The Vigil's Lanternbearers were failed Keys. **T3: Ivel was the previous Key**; he became the heart of the Gloam to hold the lid. Your Act II choice mattered: `ember` thinned the lid (sun woke sooner), `hunger` thickened it (the lid is stronger but starving the world). The Hollowed in the Gloamheart, the Snuffer, the Unwritten: forty previous Keys, spent.
- **T4: Maelis, the Warden quest-giver, is the Ninth Lamplighter**, four hundred years old, the last of the order. She has been sending Keys down for centuries, and sent you. She is not a villain: she is a jailer who has run out of prisoners' patience, and she loves you the way you love a tool you have kept a long time. She wants you to become the 41st seal, and she will fight you to make sure of it.
- **T5: the Radiant speaks in your own voice**, and it is not lying: it offers you a face.
- Final choice (event `sun_choice` after the Radiant): **Rekindle** (wake it fully: day returns, harsh and total: ending `everlight`), **Reseal** (become the lock, the Long Night continues, people live in the dim: ending `longnight`), **Wear the Sun** (put on the Radiant's mask-face: the faceless Key finally has a face, a small tame sun: ending `sunface`).
Recurring image: the nine lamps, glass, ash, orreries, faceless masks, a black sun. Tone: melancholy, tragic, cosmic, intimate. New zones are lit by strange light, never churches.
